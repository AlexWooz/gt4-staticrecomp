# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc11 (test build)

**A test release candidate of 1.3.0: on CoreGS 2.0 the car in front no longer shows sharp through a Skyline R34's rear glass,
and the launcher's About credits the people who helped build GT4Recomp on GitHub. The game itself is unchanged.**

## Highlights

- **Car glass on CoreGS 2.0:** close behind a Skyline R34, the car in front showed sharp and dark through its rear glass. The glass
  is now misted over it, as on GSdx.
- **GitHub contributors in About:** Kaezzey, who started the project, and everyone with a merged or open pull request,
  credited by name.

## Fixes

- **The car in front through an R34's rear glass (CoreGS 2.0, every scale; reported by the owner on rc10).** GT4 clears the
  256 x 256 picture that cars reflect (the environment map) with two 64-pixel-wide rectangles whose depth buffer lies in the same
  memory, one page to the right: on the PlayStation 2, half of the map gets its transparency value from that depth write. CoreGS
  2.0 kept the depth apart from the picture, so that half stayed fully transparent; a car's reflection then left a transparency of
  0 on the car, and the R34's glass reflection, which is scaled by what lies behind the glass, added nothing over the car in front.
  A depth fill now writes the picture whose memory it covers, block by block, as the console does. On the owner's recording at 6x
  the glass looks as on GSdx; on the other recorded races only some car glass changes, by at most 133 pixels of a frame, and closer
  to GSdx or equal.

## The same game

Against rc9 (the gated reference; rc10 matched it everywhere), on this build:

- **Physics:** the race clock at every VBlank and the whole simulation state, byte for byte, on three recorded laps (George V Paris,
  El Capitan, Tokyo R246) and on the v2.00 night race with 5 AI cars.
- **Frames on v2.00:** 10 of 10 at 3x (night race) and 14 of 14 (Arcade path) identical.
- **Frames on Spec II:** only car glass changes, by a few pixels: 18 of 24, 26 of 35 and 17 of 35 frames of the three laps at 1x,
  and 12 of 12 at 6x by 8 to 32 pixels of about 10 million. With the fix switched off (`GT4_CORE2_DEPTH_INTO_COLOUR=0`) the El
  Capitan lap is identical again (35 of 35).
- **Checks while running, against the exact interpreter:** 236,448 VU1 samples on Spec II and 355,388 on v2.00, and the MTVU check
  over 8,032 frames, all with 0 differences.

## Install or update

- **From rc8, rc9, rc10 or another 1.3.0 test build:** the launcher offers rc11 on the Beta channel (Settings > General).
- **Installer:** run `GT4Recomp-Setup-1.3.0-rc11.exe`. It keeps existing settings, saves and replays.
- **Portable:** unpack `GT4Recomp-1.3.0-rc11-portable.zip` anywhere. Settings, saves and data stay inside the folder.
- **After the update,** Play may compile CoreGS 2.0's shaders once, as after every update.
- **Disc images:** you need your own: Gran Turismo 4 (USA, v2.00), and your own Spec II 1.10 image for the mod. GT4Recomp ships no
  copyrighted game files.

## Known issues and limitations in this test build

- **Custom textures:** lap time digits that a player saw without the pack's image in a race with opponents were not reproduced on
  the recordings (they are drawn from the pack on the Time Trial).
- **A Spec II replay renamed by the launcher** (only when two different replays share a name) gets GT4's four-digit form; whether
  Spec II lists it is not verified.
- **Everything else** is unchanged from rc10, including its known issues.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a processor with SSE4.1. CoreGS 2.0 needs a graphics card with Vulkan 1.2.
