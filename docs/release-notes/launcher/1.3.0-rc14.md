# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc14

**Zoom the race camera in and out, finer throttle and brake by default, Spec II's speed text fixed on wide screens, a
first-start hint for creating your save, and a fix for games that closed while preparing shaders.**

## New
- In a race, hold R3 and press up or down on the D-pad to zoom the camera in or out; L3 + R3 goes back to normal (both games, on
  by default).
- The throttle and brake now follow the whole trigger travel in about 255 steps instead of the game's 46 and 85 (on by default;
  Controls > "Full trigger range" turns it off).
- The first time you play a game with no save yet, Play tells you which key confirms the memory card questions (W or Enter on
  the keyboard, A on a controller, by default).
- Spec II: any disc image built on Spec II 1.10 (translations, car packs such as PT-BR + CPSP) installs; the ones we tested
  show "Tested", the others "Not tested". Other Spec II versions are refused.

## Fixes
- If your graphics driver closed the game while CoreGS 2.0 prepared its shaders, the next start no longer fails the same way:
  it prepares them one at a time and leaves out the one that failed.
- On screens wider than 16:9 (21:9, 32:9), the pre-race screens, the Time Trial lobby and the pause menu keep their shape,
  centred, instead of stretching (both games).
- Spec II on screens wider than 16:9: the speedometer, the odometer and the lap timer keep their shape instead of stretching, as
  in Gran Turismo 4.
- With the 360 camera on, a throttle or brake set on the left stick or on a wheel works again; only the right stick's up and
  down stop accelerating and braking.
- Controls: the trigger meters keep their size while you press the triggers.

## Good to know
- Exported logs now include the shader preparation log, which helps us find graphics driver problems.
