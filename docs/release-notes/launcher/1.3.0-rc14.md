# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc14

**Zoom the race camera in and out, an optional finer trigger range, Spec II's speed text fixed on wide screens, and a first-start
hint for creating your save.**

## New
- In a race, hold R3 and press up or down on the D-pad to zoom the camera in or out; L3 + R3 goes back to normal (both games, on
  by default).
- Controls: "Full trigger range (experimental)" makes the throttle and brake follow the whole trigger travel in about 255 steps
  instead of the game's 46 and 85 (off by default).
- The first time you play a game with no save yet, Play tells you which key confirms the memory card questions (W or Enter on
  the keyboard, A on a controller, by default).
- Spec II: the Brazilian Portuguese disc image with Gran Turismo PSP cars (PT-BR + CPSP) is recognised by name.

## Fixes
- Spec II on screens wider than 16:9 (21:9, 32:9): the speedometer, the odometer and the lap timer keep their shape instead of
  stretching, as in Gran Turismo 4.
- With the 360 camera on, a throttle or brake set on the left stick or on a wheel works again; only the right stick's up and
  down stop accelerating and braking.
