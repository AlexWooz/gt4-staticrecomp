# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc13

**A 360-degree chase camera on the right stick, CoreGS 2.0 fixes for photo mode, the Nordschleife and the used-car showroom,
your own texture folder, and fixes players asked for on GitHub.**

## New
- In the chase view, push the right stick left or right to turn the camera round your car (on by default, in both games).
- Graphics: choose the folder your texture packs live in (for example PCSX2's), with no copy.
- Saves: the active save can be deleted, and it no longer comes back at the next Play.
- If GSdx cannot be downloaded, try again or install it from a file you downloaded elsewhere.

## Fixes
- On CoreGS 2.0, photo mode no longer flickers to black, and the Nordschleife's far fences look right again.
- On CoreGS 2.0, the used-car showroom no longer stutters while the car turns (graphics card waits per frame from 1.6 ms to
  almost none on an RTX 3060 laptop).
- PCSX2 memory cards whose saves sit near the end of the card now import.
- With GSdx, ReShade's Home key works again, and GSdx starts when your Documents folder is in OneDrive.
- On Steam Deck and Proton, less crackling in the sound.

## Good to know
- While the 360 camera is on, the right stick no longer works as throttle and brake; the triggers do.
