# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc12

**Precise triggers and sticks, a choice of controller, no more desktop flash every few seconds in fullscreen, and on CoreGS 2.0 a race keeps its full speed when the PC cannot hold 60 FPS.**

## New
- Controls: when several controllers are connected, choose the one the game uses. At every start the launcher selects the one you chose last time, or else the first one connected.
- On CoreGS 2.0, 60 FPS lets the frame rate drop in a race when the PC cannot keep up, instead of slowing the game down. The physics stay the same, and with time to spare every frame is drawn (experimental; menus stay as before).

## Fixes
- Triggers: throttle and brake reach 100% only at the end of the trigger's travel. Before, the game was already at full throttle with about a third of the trigger.
- Sticks: steering follows the stick from a small dead zone to full lock. Before, the first half of the travel did nothing and the rest turned too fast.
- In fullscreen, mainly on NVIDIA RTX cards, the desktop or the window behind the game no longer flashes for a frame every few seconds, with a stutter. This happened on CoreGS 2.0 and GSdx alike.
- After Alt+Tab, the game no longer comes back as a white "Not responding" window.
- On CoreGS 2.0 with NVIDIA cards, the game no longer risks closing by itself in a race with the 30 FPS mode or when the frame rate drops.
