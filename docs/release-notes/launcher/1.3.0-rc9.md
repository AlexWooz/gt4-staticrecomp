# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc9

**Less work for your processor and a cleaner picture on CoreGS 2.0: about 26% less processor per frame than rc8, with the same physics.**

## Fixes
- The time trial ghost is see-through again, drawn in alternating lines as on a real PS2.
- No more black blocks or broken asphalt on Tokyo R246.
- The wrong frame as a race starts and in the fade when you leave it is gone (missing signs, white trees, El Capitan's grey road).
- No more green screens or old menus flashing when a race returns to the menus.
- Car wheels spin with their motion blur (before, the spokes looked still).

## Performance
- About 26% less processor work per frame than 1.3.0-rc8 on a Ryzen 7 5700X, in a race on George V Paris with CoreGS 2.0 at 1x: from 13.0 ms to 9.6 ms (automated runs).
- On an i5-7200U laptop the first part of this work already measured about 18% less (automated runs).
- The graphics' vertex math takes about 16% less time and the physics' vector math about a quarter less, with the same results.
- The game's own translated code runs about a third faster, thanks to a build tuned from real races, and Spec II no longer interprets part of its math.

## Good to know
- Nothing changes in the game itself: over whole laps on three tracks the race clock and the car physics stayed identical to the previous version, byte for byte.
- This test build needs no new setup: your settings and saves stay as they are.
