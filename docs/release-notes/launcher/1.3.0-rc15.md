# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc15

**Racing wheels work natively: the game sees a Logitech Driving Force Pro with its own force feedback, set up on a new
Wheel page. Also a choice of performance HUD, smoother play on the Steam Deck and fewer shader crashes.**

## New
- Racing wheels: the game now sees your wheel as a Logitech Driving Force Pro, with 900 degrees of steering and the game's own
  force feedback on your wheel.
- Settings > Wheel: the on-screen wheel turns with yours and the pedals show their travel. Known wheels are mapped for you,
  any other in a short guided setup; pedal dead zones, force strength and a Test force button.
- Performance HUD: choose Off, FPS only or Full. Full shows your graphics card and processor names in full, and whether the
  performance report is recording.
- Steam Deck and other graphics cards with a single graphics queue: the picture is shown on its own thread, so waiting for the
  screen no longer holds up the game (Settings > Graphics, on by default).

## Fixes
- If the graphics driver closes the game while CoreGS 2.0 prepares its shaders, the launcher now tries again by itself and
  shows the attempt; a shader is left out only after it fails twice.
- CoreGS 2.0 now turns on every graphics card feature it uses, and no longer refuses cards that lack formats it never needs.

## Performance
- A little less processor work in races: about 0.3 ms per frame at 1x on a recorded Paris lap (RTX 3060 laptop).

## Good to know
- This is a test build to try racing wheels. So far the wheel has been tested on one setup; tell us how yours behaves.
- Exported logs now include a fuller system report (processor, memory, Windows or Proton, graphics driver, screens,
  controllers and wheels, with no names or serial numbers) and your wheel settings.
