# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc9 (test build)

**A test release candidate of 1.3.0 for the processor and CoreGS 2.0's picture. The game side (EE, VU, IOP, runtime), the
renderer's processor work and the build around them use about a quarter less processor than rc8, with the game itself unchanged;
five picture bugs of CoreGS 2.0 found on recorded drives are fixed. It adds no options.**

## What changes

Numbers are the owner's recorded lap on George V Paris, Spec II, CoreGS 2.0 at 1x, headless, in a player's session: no report on
and CoreGS 2.0's pipelines read from a shader cache, as the launcher keeps them. Milliseconds of processor per VBlank for the whole
process, Ryzen 7 5700X and RX 9060 XT, three interleaved rounds:

| Build | Process CPU | Wall per VBlank without pacing |
|---|---|---|
| 1.3.0-rc8 | 12.96 ms | 5.99 ms |
| this build | **9.57 ms (-26%)** | 5.15 ms (-14%) |

On an i5-7200U / HD 620 the first part of this work (PGO of the whole game, the Spec II VU0 programs compiled, the VU0 macro
operations over four lanes) measured 33.87 -> 27.64 ms (-18%), headless and without a shader cache; this build was not measured
there.

**Processor work:**

- **Profile-guided optimisation and ThinLTO of the whole game,** where rc8 had them only on the GS libraries: VU AOT code, runtime,
  VU1 worker, host loop, and the EE library rebuilt from the same translated units with one compiler and SSE4.1. Trained on full
  races of each game.
- **The VU programs, regenerated by a faster emitter, with the same results:** the VU1 outer product as one vector operation, the
  VU0 programs over four lanes at once, and partial loads and stores written as whole qwords; the VU1 CLIP flags compared over the
  lanes at once. The VU1 vertex programs take about 16% less time, the VU0 programs about 28% less. Checked against the exact
  interpreter while running: 236,448 VU1 samples and 39,731 VU0 samples on Spec II, 355,388 and 56,156 on v2.00, 0 differences.
- **The exact PS2 FPU maths:** the VU0 macro operations compute four lanes at once with SSE4.1, and `fp::add`, `fp::mul` lost the
  branches a processor cannot predict. The same bits for every combination of exponents, 400 million random pairs, and the macro
  operations against their definition (`native_vu_fast_ops`).
- **Spec II VU0 microcode runs compiled:** it ran in the exact interpreter on most calls, because only v2.00 had its programs
  generated.
- **CoreGS 2.0:** work goes to the graphics card every 64 draw batches instead of 32 on a discrete card (an integrated one keeps
  32), and the vertices of each primitive are written at once.
- **The measuring itself:** with the performance report off, a session reads no clock for the F7/F8 scopes, the renderer's stage
  times or the draw pass timestamps. With it on, the scopes read the processor's counter directly. The stage times in the `[core2]`
  lines read 0 without the report, and the F7/F8 report needs Settings' performance report on, as before.

**CoreGS 2.0 picture fixes (found on the owner's recorded drives, compared with GSdx):**

- The time trial ghost is drawn in alternating lines, as on a real PS2 and GSdx; it was solid.
- Tokyo R246: no more black blocks and broken asphalt (a texture memo skipped textures that a processor-drawn sprite had changed).
- The wrong frame as a race opens and in the fade when it ends (missing signs, white trees) and El Capitan's grey road: GT4 clears
  its depth in an unusual way that was not applied.
- No more green screens or stale menus when a race returns to the 480i menus.
- On v2.00 the wheels spin with their blur, as on GSdx.
- The dark rectangle that covered part of a track sign for one frame (George V Paris lap, VBlank 3750, 2 runs of 12 on rc8 and
  rc9) did not appear in 12 runs of this build; probably the same stale texture identities as Tokyo's blocks.

## Same game

Against the build with the picture fixes before this round's processor work (slice392), on this build:

- **Physics:** the race clock at every VBlank and the whole simulation state at VBlank 8000, byte for byte, on three recorded laps
  (George V Paris, El Capitan, Tokyo R246); the same on the v2.00 night race with 5 AI cars, state at VBlank 10000.
- **Frames:** 24, 35 and 35 of the three laps at 1x; 10 of 10 at 3x (v2.00 night race), 14 of 14 (v2.00 Arcade path), 12 of 12 at

- **Checks while running:** the VU1 and VU0 checks above; the MTVU check over 8,032 frames, 0 differences.

## Known issues and limitations in this test build

- **Not measured in a real session yet:** all of the above is headless. The owner's test on the desktop comes next; the notebook

- **Everything else** is unchanged from rc8, including its known issues.

## Install or test

- **Installer:** run `GT4Recomp-Setup-1.3.0-rc9.exe`. It keeps existing settings, saves and replays.
- **Portable:** unpack `GT4Recomp-1.3.0-rc9-portable.zip` anywhere. Settings, saves and data stay inside the folder.
- **Disc images:** you need your own: Gran Turismo 4 (USA, v2.00), and your own Spec II 1.10 image for the mod. GT4Recomp ships no
  copyrighted game files.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a processor with SSE4.1. CoreGS 2.0 needs a graphics card with Vulkan 1.2.
