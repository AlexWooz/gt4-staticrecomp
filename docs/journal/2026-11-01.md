## The readback before a write on George V Paris (IN-508, candidate 370)

The owner asked to reduce the readback cost at 6x, starting from the handoff's "Next step".

- **The finding, checked again on candidate 369 (Confirmed):** a new trace gave the same stores: CT32 at BP 12224, BW 1, 64 x 1184 (0.73 per VBlank) and 64 x 1440 (0.26), over the Z32 target at BP 11232, BW 4, drawn 0,0,191,255. A separate page calculation gave drawn pages 351 to 381 (24 pages), written pages from 382, no page in common. Stores 1.28 per VBlank, readbacks 5.62.
- **The proposed test was not enough (Confirmed):** with only page-aligned bases the big upload stopped storing, but the small textures that follow it in page 382 (`bp=12232 8x8`, 0.42 per VBlank; `bp=12226 8x2`, 0.11) took over the store, because their bases are inside a page: 0.86 stores per VBlank, still 0.58 of the three-target readback. `PagesOfRect` now walks block cells for such a base. After that: 0.28 stores and 2.63 readbacks per VBlank, and the three targets are no longer read back for this write.
- **The dirty side:** only the target's touched pages, whole and in its format, as spec 4.1 says. They count as written memory as before, and the target is not replaced by such a write. With this side turned off on purpose, the new test fails with 9 failures: the drawn pixels come back old.
- **A defect found on the way (Confirmed in the test, Unknown in the game):** a drawn target nothing has read yet is not stored before a write, so whole-row patches and the 4.1 deletion lost its drawn pixels: 6,352 bytes off the CPU reference in the test with the switch off, 0 with it on. The 78 game frames do not show it, so these scenes do not reach it or do not display it.
- **A test trap:** at 2x a target's undrawn first sub-pixel row and column hold earlier content, which the old whole-row patch happened to refresh. The new test samples inside the sprite. The game frames at 3x, 6x and 12x did not change.
