The GS thread figures are not the same measure: GSdx's is one CPU thread's busy time as its stream statistics report it, CoreGS 2.0's is all the work of its thread, GPU waits included (5.5 and 5.0 ms). GSdx's GPU time is not in these lines. Hypothesis: the EE's 9.13 ms in `run_due_events` under CoreGS 2.0 Performance Mode is the EE waiting for the GS thread to catch up; `worker_to_ee` waits are 281 per frame there against 0 under GSdx.
