## The readback before a write on George V Paris (IN-508, candidate 370)

The owner asked to reduce the readback cost at 6x, starting from the handoff's "Next step".

- **The finding, checked again on candidate 369 (Confirmed):** a new trace gave the same stores: CT32 at BP 12224, BW 1, 64 x 1184 (0.73 per VBlank) and 64 x 1440 (0.26), over the Z32 target at BP 11232, BW 4, drawn 0,0,191,255. A separate page calculation gave drawn pages 351 to 381 (24 pages), written pages from 382, no page in common. Stores 1.28 per VBlank, readbacks 5.62.
- **The proposed test was not enough (Confirmed):** with only page-aligned bases the big upload stopped storing, but the small textures that follow it in page 382 (`bp=12232 8x8`, 0.42 per VBlank; `bp=12226 8x2`, 0.11) took over the store, because their bases are inside a page: 0.86 stores per VBlank, still 0.58 of the three-target readback. `PagesOfRect` now walks block cells for such a base. After that: 0.28 stores and 2.63 readbacks per VBlank, and the three targets are no longer read back for this write.
- **The dirty side:** only the target's touched pages, whole and in its format, as spec 4.1 says. They count as written memory as before, and the target is not replaced by such a write. With this side turned off on purpose, the new test fails with 9 failures: the drawn pixels come back old.
- **A defect found on the way (Confirmed in the test, Unknown in the game):** a drawn target nothing has read yet is not stored before a write, so whole-row patches and the 4.1 deletion lost its drawn pixels: 6,352 bytes off the CPU reference in the test with the switch off, 0 with it on. The 78 game frames do not show it, so these scenes do not reach it or do not display it.
- **A test trap:** at 2x a target's undrawn first sub-pixel row and column hold earlier content, which the old whole-row patch happened to refresh. The new test samples inside the sprite. The game frames at 3x, 6x and 12x did not change.

## The owner's F8 on candidate 370, and the frametime wave (IN-509, candidate 371)

The owner tested 370 at 6x: "much better performance", but a horrible frame pacing in both modes: at a stable 60 FPS the frametime has many peaks, like an audio waveform, with frames of 10-12 ms in RivaTuner beside others over 20 ms, plus stutters at the race start in Performance Mode. The owner said it was already there before this version.

| Owner's F8 on candidate 370, George V Paris at 6x | No Performance Mode | Performance Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Session | session-1793511403 | session-1793511564 |
| VBlanks/s | 59.20 | 59.45 |
| Frame median / p95 / p99 / max | 16.68 / 21.59 / 23.27 / 41.15 | 16.63 / 23.30 / 24.21 / 42.96 |
| Intervals under 15 ms | 1684 of 5657 | 878 of 6088 |
| Intervals of 20 ms and more | 1148 of 5657 | 704 of 6088 |

- **The clue:** the spike lines came at every second VBlank (`gap 2`: 219 of 337 and 358 of 491), with `created=9` and six readbacks in those VBlanks. The log caps at 40 such lines per 600 VBlanks, so the real count is higher; `GT4_CORE2_SPIKE_LINES` now raises it.
- **Reproduced headless on 370 (Confirmed):** GS thread 18.4 ms then 13.5 ms, alternating. A new trace line names the target: `keep vblank=20801 target bp=0 bw=4 psm=49 size=256x256 last_draw_vblank=20799`. `DrawTarget` drops a target of another width unless it was drawn in this VBlank or the last, and GT4 draws this one every second VBlank, after drawing the same memory at other widths. The drop stores it through `KeepGpuReadContent`, which reads back every target over its span, four of them 640 x 448.
- **Why the owner's peaks are larger:** the headless readback waits 0.5 ms for the GPU; in the owner's window 7 to 11 ms. So the same VBlank costs about 5 ms headless and 10 to 15 ms in the window.
- **The fix:** two VBlanks of grace. The target stays beside the others, is not recreated, and its memory is stored only when something asks for it.
- **Not the cause:** `FramePacer` catches up after a late VBlank, which is why a 22 ms frame is followed by a short one; that is the visible half of the wave, not its source. It was left as it is.
- **A 97 ms maximum in the first summary:** one VBlank of 261 ms in one run, every phase slower by the same factor, no readback: interference from outside, not in the other five Performance Mode runs. Those two rounds are the ones in the table.
