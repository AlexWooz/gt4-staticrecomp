## The readback before a write on George V Paris (IN-508, candidate 370)

The owner asked to reduce the readback cost at 6x, starting from the handoff's "Next step".

- **The finding, checked again on candidate 369 (Confirmed):** a new trace gave the same stores: CT32 at BP 12224, BW 1, 64 x 1184 (0.73 per VBlank) and 64 x 1440 (0.26), over the Z32 target at BP 11232, BW 4, drawn 0,0,191,255. A separate page calculation gave drawn pages 351 to 381 (24 pages), written pages from 382, no page in common. Stores 1.28 per VBlank, readbacks 5.62.
- **The proposed test was not enough (Confirmed):** with only page-aligned bases the big upload stopped storing, but the small textures that follow it in page 382 (`bp=12232 8x8`, 0.42 per VBlank; `bp=12226 8x2`, 0.11) took over the store, because their bases are inside a page: 0.86 stores per VBlank, still 0.58 of the three-target readback. `PagesOfRect` now walks block cells for such a base. After that: 0.28 stores and 2.63 readbacks per VBlank, and the three targets are no longer read back for this write.
- **The dirty side:** only the target's touched pages, whole and in its format, as spec 4.1 says. They count as written memory as before, and the target is not replaced by such a write. With this side turned off on purpose, the new test fails with 9 failures: the drawn pixels come back old.
- **A defect found on the way (Confirmed in the test, Unknown in the game):** a drawn target nothing has read yet is not stored before a write, so whole-row patches and the 4.1 deletion lost its drawn pixels: 6,352 bytes off the CPU reference in the test with the switch off, 0 with it on. The 78 game frames do not show it, so these scenes do not reach it or do not display it.
- **A test trap:** at 2x a target's undrawn first sub-pixel row and column hold earlier content, which the old whole-row patch happened to refresh. The new test samples inside the sprite. The game frames at 3x, 6x and 12x did not change.

- **The clue:** the spike lines came at every second VBlank (`gap 2`: 219 of 337 and 358 of 491), with `created=9` and six readbacks in those VBlanks. The log caps at 40 such lines per 600 VBlanks, so the real count is higher; `GT4_CORE2_SPIKE_LINES` now raises it.
- **Reproduced headless on 370 (Confirmed):** GS thread 18.4 ms then 13.5 ms, alternating. - **The rolling start (IN-510):** reproduced headless, VBlank 8826 at 37 ms. GT4 loads 8 palettes (CBP 13696 to 13724) 140 times each in a VBlank, from a colour and depth pair at one base, and each load wrote both targets into the GLM with nothing changed. The first memo failed because the two targets invalidated each other, which the trace showed. A memo of the last set worked; the final one remembers each target's page rows, with newest draw wins.
- **Why "not always":** in the owner's sessions the signature appears in some stretches (`glm_writes` 2,600 to 3,700 per VBlank). What makes the game enter that loop is Unknown.
- **The showroom wave (IN-512):** every second VBlank a 24 x 12 T4 upload lands inside a drawn CT32 page. This overlap is real, so IN-508 cannot help. Sequence of attempts, all kept in the tests:
  - patching only the written bits failed `hw_core2_patch_bits` on the cache stream: with two targets over one page the newer one also needs the older one's pixels;
  - the merge on the GPU passes: targets into the GLM oldest first, the touched blocks patched from there, the written bits on top;
  - a crash: `BlockGrid` has no cells for Z formats, so depth targets keep the old path;
  - stores then moved to other writes: older dirty rectangles and a 304 x 34 write kept them eligible only after applying the dirty rectangles first and widening the limits;
  - the merge also took writes that needed no store, so it now runs only when a store would.
- **At 12x:** one frame of 31 differs in 2 pixels. Three more runs show it is deterministic, and turning `GT4_CORE2_PATCH_BITS` off restores it. Accepted as a named relaxation, since the 1x gate holds and the old whole-row patch was the less exact of the two.
- **Dropped with a measurement:** the exact page test for textures. Decodes over targets 391,334 to 381,065 and no gain: those overlaps are real. Reverted.
- **Found instead (IN-513):** one VBlank had 353 decodes over targets of 43 distinct textures, one of them 148 times, and 528 GLM writes of 24 distinct. Reuse inside the VBlank while memory and the targets are unchanged.
- **A tool stop:** a safety check refused a recursive removal of an old evidence folder; a new folder was used instead, and the old evidence folders under `build/evidence` were left in place.
