## TASK-204: CoreGS 2.0 never compiles a pipeline during play (branch `task/204-no-ingame-compile`)

Owner brief from a player crash on rc15: RX 6750 XT, AMD driver 25.10.30.02, CoreGS 2.0 at 4x, `0xC0000005` inside a
pipeline compile on the `spec_worker` thread. The private logs (`crash-log-1532`) were read for the diagnosis and never
copied into the repository.

**Read from the player's `game.log`:** 301 variants recreated at start in 227.7 ms (0.75 ms each: disk-cache hits); then
44 new keys requested during play, `compile_ms` 227.7 -> 26,517.1 ms with `compile_max_ms` 1,047.9 ms; `compiling.bin`
held the key in flight when the process died; `quarantine.bin` had 6 keys from earlier prepares (the launcher had run 14
attempts in an earlier session). So the TASK-170 guard and the TASK-170b retry loop covered the prepare only, and
`PreparedStamp` did not hash `variants.bin`, so a session that recorded new keys left the prepare reporting "ready".

**What changed (native).** `native/hw_gs/core2/core2_gpu.cpp`:
- `specialised_pipeline` no longer compiles an unknown key: it records it in `variants.bin` (through `specKnown`), counts
  it in `statDeferred`/`specDeferredBatches`, and returns null so the batch keeps the uber pipeline. A repeat of a
  deferred key is counted too. The switch `GT4_CORE2_INGAME_COMPILE=1` restores the old path; without
  `GT4_HW_SHADER_CACHE` there is no list to defer to.
- `queue_known_variants` recreates the recorded variants only when `prepared_for(prepared_stamp())` is true, so a play
  session only asks the driver for what the prepare compiled (a disk-cache hit); otherwise it logs the missing prepare
  and keeps uber.
- `PreparedStamp` gained `variants` (FNV-1a of the keys) and `variantCount`; the prepare writes the stamp of the list it
  compiled, so a recorded key makes the next Play prepare again. `variants_to_prepare` reports how many keys came from the
  sessions' list.
- `open_compile_guard`: a key left in `compiling.bin` is quarantined at once when the game starts (a play strike); the
  prepare keeps TASK-170c's two strikes.
- `[core2] specialised pipelines=` gained `deferred=N deferred_uber_batches=M`; the prepare line names the recorded keys.
- `Core2Counters` gained `specPipelines`, `specDeferred`, `specDeferredBatches`, `uberBatches` for the fixtures.

**What changed (launcher).** `gt4l-core/src/launch.rs` parses `shaders recorded R`;
`gt4l-platform/src/game.rs` carries the count out of every attempt; `gt4l-tauri/src/commands.rs` logs
`shaders: R recorded keys compiled`. The Play flow already runs the prepare every time and opens the progress window on
the first `shaders progress 0 T`, which the stamp change now makes happen when `variants.bin` grew.

**Tests.**
- New `hw_core2_no_ingame_compile`: play records 6 variants, creates 0 pipelines, 55 uber batches all caused by them, and
  5 of 5 frames equal the `GT4_CORE2_INGAME_COMPILE=1` run (6 pipelines); the stamp is ready after a prepare, does no
  work twice, and works again after `variants.bin` grew by one key; a play strike leaves `compiling.bin`, the prepare then
  sees 1 suspect, and a play start quarantines it (1 quarantined, 0 suspects).
- `hw_core2_shader_cache` reworked to the product flow: session records (6 deferred, 0 pipelines) -> prepare (306
  variants, 6 recorded, `pipelines.bin` 2,794,892 bytes) -> second session recreates them from the disk cache with the
  same pictures.
- `hw_core2_*` 35 of 35 pass (also `hw_core2_upload_swizzle` and `hw_core2_depth_alias`).
- Launcher `cargo test --workspace`: 783 passed, 9 ignored (the rc15 baseline; the only warnings are pre-existing unused
  imports in `moves.rs`, `discord_presence.rs` and `tray.rs`).

**Headless gate (v2.00 night race, `scripts/coregs2_bench/night-run.sh` scene, 1x).** Three runs in

Frames: `on` vs `off` **9 of 9 identical** (SHA-256 per frame), `prepared` vs `off` **9 of 9 identical**, `prepared2` vs

**Build.** `private/slice414-build` (the rc15 recipe with `-DGT4_ASSETS_ROOT=C:/Antigravity/gt4-staticrecomp`, EE profile

## TASK-204 revision: independent review, checks rerun and the 1.3.0-rc16 test build

Continuation of the TASK-204 note above, on the same branch and worktree.

**Review.** `git diff origin/release/1.3.0-rc15...HEAD` was re-read against decision 0078. `specialised_pipeline` has no
driver-compile path while `compiles_in_play()` is false: an unknown key enters `specDeferred` and `variants.bin` exactly
once (the `specDeferred` check comes before `specSeen`), the repeated draw only increments `specDeferredBatches`, and the
worker is started with no lock held and joined before the destructor saves the cache. `GT4_CORE2_INGAME_COMPILE=1` and a
run without `GT4_HW_SHADER_CACHE` keep the old path, still under `begin_compile`/`end_compile`. `PreparedStamp` has a
defined value for an absent, empty or damaged `variants.bin` (empty list, FNV-1a offset basis, count 0), and a damaged
`prepared.bin` only makes `prepared_for` false. One strike in play quarantines, the prepare keeps TASK-170c's two strikes,
and a quarantined key is put into `specSeen`, so it is never recorded again (no prepare/prepare-again loop). The
launcher parser needs exactly `shaders recorded N`, and a failed or absent prepare cannot block Play (`prepare_shaders`
returns `Ok(true)` on every non-cancel path). **Fixed in review:** three stale comments and log texts in
`gt4l-tauri/src/commands.rs` still said the game "compiles as it plays"; they now say it draws those variants with the
uber pipeline and records them for the next prepare. **Residual risk (Hypothesis, not changed, a design decision):**
`prepared_for` checks only the `pipelines.bin` header; a cache whose header matches but whose body the driver rejects
would be treated as prepared and compiled at the start. Proving it needs a hash of `pipelines.bin` in the stamp.

## TASK-205: every CoreGS 2.0 shader variant, and what creates one (branch `task/205-variant-coverage`)

**Why.** The shipped list (`core2_known_variants.inc`, 544 keys) is the union of recorded scenes, not every draw state
(IN-507). A player on rc15 at 4x asked 44 new keys in one lap and the driver ended the process inside one of the
compiles; TASK-204 now records an unknown key and draws it with the uber pipeline, and the next prepare compiles it. The
owner asked what creates a new key, for all of them mapped, and for the player base to close the gap. He also suspects
the first-encounter uber path is the occasional lag.

**What was measured.** A plain Spec II build from this branch (`private/slice416-spec2-build`, `GT4_TARGET=spec2`,
`GT4_EE_PROFILE=spec2`, the rc15 recipe, no PGO/ThinLTO) with a fresh shader cache per run, so TASK-204 records every
draw state and compiles nothing. The TASK-171 harness was copied to `private/task205-tools/`. 52 Arcade tracks (45
B-Spec with the Ford GT LM; the 7 Dirt & Snow in A-Spec with slot 13, because B-Spec never starts a race there), three
A-Spec repeats, four car slots on two tracks, the boot/Arcade/car-setup screens, and a 4x diagnostic on George V Paris.
Two new tools: `scripts/coregs2_bench/variant_decode.py` (fields, push bits, diffs) and `variant_survey.py` (merge,
dedupe, shader-set check, cap, candidate list).

**The pattern.** A key is a draw state, not a scene. The 544 keys are 144 draw-state signatures expanded over scales
1/2/3/6; 105 signatures are only at 1x and there is no 4x key. The push word carries the sampled texture's scale
(bits 0-19) and the scaled-frame flags (28/30), so every render scale is a separate variant universe: the 300
storage-path keys this card prepares project to 136 keys at 4x, all new. The player's 44 keys are the first slice of
that set. The second source is material vocabulary the recorded scenes never used: mip sampling, SCANSMK (0 keys in the
list, IN-530), region-clamp wraps, alpha-test/blend combinations, raw CT24. The 52 tracks added no new 30-field value,
only the SCANSMK flag and combinations; the car slots added one value, `Tfx=Highlight2`. No key field is a per-draw
value and nothing grows without bound (scale 1-12, aniso 0-7, ROP bounded); the design issue is that the scale
multiplies the corpus (144 signatures x 12 scales = 1,728, over the 2048 cap with aniso/ROP twins). Two proposals, not
applied: prepare-side scale expansion (launcher passes the session scale, the prepare adds the twins, no shader
change) and taking the scale out of the key (owner decision).

**The survey.** 52 race runs: 155 distinct keys, 69 new, 38 new signatures, 117 of the shipped 144 signatures seen,
4,046 repeated keys across runs, 15 keys unique to one track. All 67 1x runs: 160 keys, 72 new, 41 signatures. The
union with the shipped list is 616 keys (372 on this card after the plain/ROP filters), well under
`kMaxSpecPipelines` (2048). A-Spec adds no key beyond B-Spec; car slots 5 and 10 add 5 keys beyond the 52 tracks
(slot 0 never starts a race; slots 17/24 add none). The `variants.bin` writer saves at most every 10 s of wall time and
at the renderer's close, and a `--frames` run exits through the runtime's halt, so a run can lose its last 0-4 keys; a
rerun recovers them. Pending: GT Mode showroom, photo mode, license tests (need a Spec II card with GT Mode data),
replay and split screen (need a menu route).

**Fallback cost.** George V Paris, 4x, the same run before and after `--prepare-shaders`, repeated once: deferred
5.69 ms gpu_busy and 8.90 ms wall per VBlank against prepared 3.78 and 8.44 (1.91 ms of GPU, +50%; wall +5%). At 1x
TASK-204's night race was within noise (6.51 against 6.54). A first-encounter 4x session runs about 2 ms per frame
heavier until the next prepare, enough to cost a frame at the margin; the visible hitch was the compile (up to
1,047.9 ms), which TASK-204 removed from play.

**Player loop (design only).** A launcher card after a session with `deferred=N`, one validated export
(`variants.bin` plus the existing log bundle, with a header check and no automatic upload), the merge tool's refusal of
another build by its shader-set hash, and an en-US/pt-BR guide. No UI or launcher code.
