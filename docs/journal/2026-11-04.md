  Nenkai from Duchi/Vyerq): a `jal` of the chase camera's yaw spring sent to a routine that adds `(stick X byte - 127) * 0.0245`
  rad to f13. The "Disable right analog throttle/brake" lines supplied with Spec II are v2.00 addresses (two `beql` of
  `func_004386F0` made unconditional); in Spec II they would corrupt a `lui`. Our version: `scripts/camera360_units.py` adds a host
  call between the delay slot and the call in the translated units (the call is a direct `jal`, out of dispatch's reach), and
  `native/camera_360*` adds pi * (stick - 128) / 127 (nothing at rest) and, while on, zeroes the analog accelerator and brake
  values. Right stick X byte `0x0086F99E` confirmed by a RAM peek; right stick Y confirmed as the default analog throttle and
  brake. Headless Arcade race (Select gives the chase view): switch off identical to the unhooked build; switch on, race clock
  identical at every VBlank and the simulation identical after release (only the camera's own 30 words differ while held);
  right stick Y with the camera on is exactly coasting. Found on the way: a direct start (standalone, `game.ini`) never calls
  `gt4::frame_skip::start_from_environment()`, so `hw_frame_skip`/`hw_frame_skip_adaptive` do nothing there (not changed here).
