## TASK-142: the car in front through a Skyline R34's rear glass (CoreGS 2.0)

- **Owner's report on rc10 (6x):** close behind a car, its glass shows parts of the car in front; only behind the R34s, and it is
  the car in front, not the interior. The owner recorded opening their replay of that race start (`r34-glass`, on a copy of their
  Spec II card).
- **Narrowing:** the bug is the same at 1x, 2x and 6x, and none of CoreGS 2.0's speed switches (early Z, ROP, specialised pipelines,
  deferred draws, compute fills, ...) changes it. `GT4_CORE2_SKIP_DRAWS` showed which draws build the R34's window (the near R34 is
  drawn last in the frame; the car in front, rows 568-641, before it), and `GT4_CORE2_PROBE_DRAWS` (stored words read back after
  given draws) showed the cause: the car in front's reflection passes (Cs Ad + Cd, alpha written from the environment map) left
  alpha 0 on it, and the R34's glass reflection is scaled by the alpha behind the glass, so it added nothing there and the car
  stayed sharp and dark. On GSdx it is misted.
- **Cause:** GT4 clears its 256 x 256 environment map (11200/4) with two 64-wide sprites whose depth buffer (11232 Z32) is the
  map's own memory one page column to the right: columns 64-127 and 192-255 get alpha 0x80 from the depth write. CoreGS 2.0 kept
  that depth in its own image, so those columns stayed at alpha 0.
- **Fix (IN-535):** a depth fill now writes its words into the 32-bit colour targets whose memory it covers, block by block
  (`core2_depth_alias`, test `hw_core2_depth_alias` against `memory::pixelSlot`). The first version merged the two sprites into one
  rectangle when deciding where the colour write wins and missed columns 64-127, the ones the car read; the probe of the map showed
  it.
- **Checks:** R34 at 6x every 100 VBlanks, 8 of 13 frames change (at most 1,527 of 10.3 million pixels, on the cars), 7 closer to
  GSdx and 1 equal; Tokyo R246 at 2x every 250, 17 of 43 frames change by at most 127 pixels (car glass), all 17 closer to GSdx;
  El Capitan 6 of 43, at most 133 pixels (a Ford GT's rear glass edge), equal to GSdx within 0.01. The owner looked at the R34
  frames: fixed. Change sheets (before, after, changed pixels, GSdx) in `private/task142-sheets` (`t142sheet.py`).
