## Night: TASK-090 round 6: the strip table decoded, all 116 car draws gated, flat-shaded M2

- **The strip table is decoded.** The `V1-8` channel at TOPS 0x40 is a strip-length list: byte 0 is the strip count, each
  next byte is **three times the triangle count** of one strip (a strip has `byte / 3 + 2` vertices), and a first byte of
  0 means one strip over the whole item (the all-zero variant). Draw 3 of the night race carries `3, 6, 57, 18` for its 33
  vertices = strips of 4, 21 and 8. The earlier "32-bit mask" reading came from the decoder ignoring the UNPACK **STMASK
  enable bit (28)** and the one-component broadcast: the write lands in lane 3 of the destination qwords, and the lane-0
  read returned the neighbouring UV bytes (`176,176,84,84`). Both are fixed in `coregs3_vif`.
- **The capture and the renderer reproduce the breaks without the tap.** `build_mesh` splits each item by its own table
  (verified: every table's lengths sum to the item's vertex count; 0 refusals over 116 draws). `--list` prints the strip
  lengths; the offline renderer and the M2 pictures use them.
