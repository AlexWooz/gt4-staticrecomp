# 2026-11-18

## Tourist Trophy as a third target (contributor pull request, issue #236)

A contributor's branch adds Tourist Trophy (USA, SCUS-97502) beside v2.00 and Spec II. Summary of the work (2026-11-11 to
2026-11-13; details and evidence in `docs/TT-STUDY.md`):

- `CORE.TT` is the GT4 Online beta's container (Salsa20/20, CRC-32C) and unpacks with `scripts/spec2_beta_elf.py` unchanged;
  VU1 microcode is byte-identical to v2.00 and VU0 to Spec II, so the existing VU tables and generator apply.
- The translated library needed the entry seeds (`scripts/spec2_entry_seeds.py`): without them Options halted on a missing
  far-`lui` entry, as Spec II's did.
- Hooks were mapped by masked matching of the v2.00 functions and confirmed in guest RAM (save states of headless runs): 16:9
  hold, race display 480p, frame skipping (TT's race classes, DynamicsConductor clock at + 0x23960 as in Spec II), license
  guard, trigger range, 360-degree camera, FOV.
- License demos carry the license setup, so the TASK-017 guard alone left the rider off the course. The exact decrypt
  (a C++ port of `scripts/license_demo_oracle.py`'s beam search) is hooked into TT's cipher. TT's License 3 needed a
  `mul.s` result one ulp below the truncated product, which GT4's confirmed chains never did; with that case all 40 US
  license demos decrypt exactly (CRC, header and Recorder layout), and the contributor completed every license test.
- The launcher identifies TT by its code, prepares the ELF from `CORE.TT` in Rust, and keeps TT's saves and replays apart
  (`BASCUS-97502…`, checked with a real replay).

Not done: a pinned TT library for releases (owner's machine), Tourist Trophy mode / challenges / photo mode play-through, and
the display aspect hooks for screens other than 16:9. Possibly useful for v2.00: the low `mul.s` case may account for some of
GT4's license demos without a confirmed chain (Hypothesis).
