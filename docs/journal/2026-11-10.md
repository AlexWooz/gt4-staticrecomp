## Parallel wave: research rounds 14-16

- **Round 14 (remaining VU1 families).** All seven unlit families decoded and validated against the r13 tap: 9-qword
  first-power (0x0B48/0x0290) and power-16 (0x03D8/0x0418), 3-qword gain/clamp/bias (0x1508), the 6-qword no-colour
  Phong-style specular (0x0CA8) and the no-block pass-through (0x00B0). Residuals are max 1 level and alpha exact where
  the item's own block is captured. The light block is per item/material, not per draw; the v3 capture stores only one
  per draw, which explains the unmatched items. Tooling: the Python V2 format row was fixed and a VU1 simulator matches
  the AOT fragments. Evidence: `build/analysis-lighting/r14/`.
- **Round 15 (texture pipeline).** The car pass uses T4 (163/169 records), T8 (5) and one CT32 256x256 env map; CT32/CSM1
  palettes, no mipmaps, MODULATE, alpha test/basic blend. The 40-byte PGLUtexture record describes TEX0/TEX1/CLAMP; the
  capture has no pixels, palettes or per-pass GS state. The model UV is s16/4096 with no V flip (the current decoder
  divides by 65536 and flips V); the C++ tap parser misreads RGBAQ/ST/UV and lacks A+D. The smallest unblock is a GS-side
  SLM snapshot (~784 KiB/frame in the car block range) plus CoreGS 2.0's decoded textures as the reference. Evidence:
  `build/analysis-textures/`.
- **Round 16 (fog, GS state, effects).** Fog parameters are in PGL +0xFA0 (already captured): `t = clamp(u/w,0,1)`,
  `F = clamp(trunc(alpha + inverse*t),0,255)`, 99.83% within +/-1 on 4,659 vertices; FOGCOL is only in the tap, not the
  capture. The car pass uses AFAIL KEEP, DATE on the additive env-map pass, GS truncating blends, ZMSK=1 on reflections,
  no culling, COLCLAMP=1 default. The reflection is a second pass with the CT32 env map; the body fragment writes the
  reflection ST at TOPS+0x40, and the exact scale remains open. Priority: fringe-family lighting first, then fog, then
  textured paint, then the env-map reflection. Evidence: `build/analysis-effects/`.
