- **TASK-215: per-record step stopped before implementing, because the vertex conversion is ~0.** ToGpuVertex is a pure
  function of Vertex (no register/palette state), so the front end cannot precompute anything the renderer would not
  have to compute; the step moves the work and adds a store. Work injection on license B-1 1x (one extra conversion pass
  per emitted GPU vertex), 2+2: 	ranslate 0.460/0.460 against 0.460/0.450 with an ordinary 32-byte arena store (below
  noise), 0.540/0.535 against 0.770/0.755 with a forced volatile store (+0.22 ms of uncached writes), GS 8.305/8.400
  against 8.435/8.270, wall 8.730/9.165 against 9.655/8.830. The sampler puts the whole inlined writeback::Translate at
  0.135 ms/VBlank (0.405 inclusive), under the 0.2 ms bar. **Contract point that blocks the removal:** Batch::vertices
  is std::vector<Vertex> (int32 x4/y4, float q/s/t, u/v) read by the CPU reference, TextureFootprint,
  PossibleShuffle* and the front end's cull/trace, while gpu::GpuVertex is the 32-byte GPU record with x/y truncated
  to 16 bits; making the record replace Vertex is the full design. Corrected bound for that design: **0.15-0.3 ms**
  (was 0.5-1.0). Recommendation: do not spend the 2-3 days. Diagnostic removed (tree unchanged); frames identical across
  builds (license 4/4, night 2/2); CTest 319/320 with the pre-existing rc15 python_native_contracts only.
