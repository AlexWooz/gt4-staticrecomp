## Night: TASK-090 round 3, the coordinate space and the flat parts

- **The coordinate space is confirmed** from the owner's hint and our own front end (`hw_front_end.cpp:621`): GS XYZ is
  12.4 and `px = (X - XYOFFSET.OFX)/16`. A constant screen offset is absorbed by the projective fit, so the offset was
  never what blocked the fit.
- **The model decode is confirmed, and the "flat" parts are shadows.** Draw 3 of the captured frame has full 3D positions
  (`(-3042, 3413, -3207)`), while draws 1 and 2 are genuinely `y == 0`: ground shadows and decals. Rendering the body of a
  single car (`--first-car 19 --min-y 0.05`) gives a recognisable car silhouette; the noisy fill in it is the unresolved

## Round 5: the strip-break channel

- A car part carries, besides its `V3-16` positions, a `V3-8 x33` channel at TOPS 0x80 (not `V4-8`, so not the colour), a
  `V2-16 x33` channel at TOPS 0x40 (UV-like) and a `V1-8 x4` channel at TOPS 0x40 with bytes `176,176,84,84`. Four bytes
  for 33 vertices is 32 bits: the break list is a **bit mask**, not an index list. Other car shapes carry an all-zero
  `V1-8` channel, which is why some parts tear and others do not.
- The tap's ADC flags are a per-vertex draw flag (`1,1,0,0` in the observed stream), not a strip-break marker.
- The 3x identity gate was re-run after the round's changes and still passes: 8,997 VBlanks, 1,686 racing, clock/digest/state
  identical, 13/13 frames identical for `off` and `shadow`.
- M2 keeps the geometric break heuristic and is labelled as geometry evidence, not fidelity. Decoding the 32-bit mask is the
  first task of the next round.
