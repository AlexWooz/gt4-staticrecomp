## Late evening: TASK-090 phase 1 round 2, the VU1 tap and the 3x identity gate

  adapter (`worker_gif`, and the synchronous path), gated to the capture's VBlank window and bounded to 96 MB; an
  unconfigured tap is one branch. `native/coregs3/coregs3_gif.{hpp,cpp}` parses the bytes with the field layout of CoreGS
