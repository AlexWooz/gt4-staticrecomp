- **The vertex transform is identified (H).** The `0xC1` block is four columns `vf24..vf27`, used unscaled over positions
  in metres (the uploaded packet rows are `vf * cmd.value`; dividing the model positions by 4096 cancels it), with
  `screen = ((0.5 + 0.5·cx/cw)·W, (0.5 − 0.5·cy/cw)·H)`. The VU1 adds an `XYOFFSET` of about (−2368, +1824) px that the
  GS subtracts. Three hand checks (draw 0 vertices 0-1, draw 3 vertex 0) agree within 0.28 px; the gate's 97 printed fits
  are consistent but were not recomputed. Consequence: `coregs3_render.cpp`'s `draw_matrix()` (row reading, `value`
  scaling) is known-wrong in screen mode, and its published pictures all used `--object` anyway.
- **Tool:** `gt4_coregs3_tap gate … --fits` prints the per-draw fitted map, the first pairs and the draw's matrix; output
  `build/coregs3-r7/gate-fits.txt`. The gate line is unchanged (116/116 gated, 111 fitted, mean 0.9714 px, worst
