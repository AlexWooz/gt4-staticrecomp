## Night: TASK-090 round 10 — the live `present` layer (M4)

- **Frame ring:** `native/coregs3/coregs3_present.{hpp,cpp}`, 8 slots, O(1) vector swaps; the producer uses `try_lock`
  and drops rather than waiting; the consumer claims the newest complete frame; atomic counters report published,
  dropped, claims, passes, draws, vertices and triangles.
- **Live layer:** `native/hw_gs/coregs3/hw_coregs3_present.{hpp,cpp}` with `coregs3_present.vert/.frag`: one
  triangle-list draw per captured draw, the raw `vf24..vf27` columns in a push constant,
  `gl_Position = vec4(cx, -cy, 1.0, cw)`, D32 depth, `GREATER` (`depth = 1/cw`), `depthClamp`, no culling, vertex
  colour only. Inserted after `ComposeFrameGpu` in `Backend::end_frame` and `Backend::scanout`, on `Impl::queue`
  through `Impl::submit_to`.
- **Deviation from the round-7 design:** the composed display image is created without `COLOR_ATTACHMENT` usage on this
  device, so the fragment stage writes the packed R32_UINT word with an `imageStore` (storage image); the D32 depth
  attachment, `GREATER` test and `depthClamp` remain, and the image barrier is `GENERAL`↔`GENERAL`.
- **Evidence (Avell A70, RTX 3060 Laptop, Vulkan 1.4.357):** full 3x night run, 8,997 VBlanks / 1,686 racing, 13 passes,
